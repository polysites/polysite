# Example sites

One Polysite installation runs many sites, and the sites can look nothing alike. These ten are the **example themes** from the
[`polysite-themes`](https://github.com/polysites/polysite-themes) repository, each running as its own site on one installation,
with its own domain and its own content. Every name, brand, person and picture in them is a made-up placeholder, and every
picture is original artwork drawn for the purpose.

Each screenshot is the home page of a real running site at 1280 pixels wide. Open a theme's folder on GitHub for its README, its
layout and its demo content (`showcase/site.json`).

## Maison Lune

![Maison Lune: the home page of the salon example theme](/examples/salon.png)

A soft, warm theme for a day spa or beauty parlour: an arched hero, serif headings, a price list, team, gallery and booking form. Theme: [`polysite-theme-salon`](https://github.com/polysites/polysite-themes/tree/main/polysite-theme-salon).

## Café Tilleul

![Café Tilleul: the home page of the restaurant example theme](/examples/restaurant.png)

A neighbourhood restaurant or café, bilingual-ready: a menu with prices and tags, gallery, opening hours, reservations and a language switcher. Theme: [`polysite-theme-restaurant`](https://github.com/polysites/polysite-themes/tree/main/polysite-theme-restaurant).

## Fernlight Studio

![Fernlight Studio: the home page of the agency example theme](/examples/agency.png)

A confident marketing look for a consultancy or software company: big type, a gradient, pricing and testimonials. Theme: [`polysite-theme-agency`](https://github.com/polysites/polysite-themes/tree/main/polysite-theme-agency).

## Tuppence & Thistle

![Tuppence & Thistle: the home page of the shop example theme](/examples/shop.png)

A warm, paper-like look for a small local shop: a catalogue of products, opening hours and enquiries, no checkout. Theme: [`polysite-theme-shop`](https://github.com/polysites/polysite-themes/tree/main/polysite-theme-shop).

## Small Hours

![Small Hours: the home page of the blog example theme](/examples/blog.png)

A personal writer's blog: paper and ink, a serif at a comfortable measure, a journal list and long-form articles with reading time and share buttons. Theme: [`polysite-theme-blog`](https://github.com/polysites/polysite-themes/tree/main/polysite-theme-blog).

## Juno Fairweather

![Juno Fairweather: the home page of the portfolio example theme](/examples/portfolio.png)

A minimal, image-led theme for a designer or photographer: a statement hero, an irregular picture grid and project pages. Theme: [`polysite-theme-portfolio`](https://github.com/polysites/polysite-themes/tree/main/polysite-theme-portfolio).

## Mira Castellane

![Mira Castellane: the home page of the resume example theme](/examples/resume.png)

A personal CV site: one column on a sheet of paper, a timeline, skills and projects, and a print stylesheet for a clean one-page CV. Theme: [`polysite-theme-resume`](https://github.com/polysites/polysite-themes/tree/main/polysite-theme-resume).

## Sundial Weekend

![Sundial Weekend: the home page of the event example theme](/examples/event.png)

A loud poster look for a one-weekend conference or festival: schedule, speakers, tickets and sponsors. Theme: [`polysite-theme-event`](https://github.com/polysites/polysite-themes/tree/main/polysite-theme-event).

## Brookside Neighbours

![Brookside Neighbours: the home page of the nonprofit example theme](/examples/nonprofit.png)

A warm look for a community charity: donation tiers, what-we-do cards, a leader's quote and a volunteer sign-up. Theme: [`polysite-theme-nonprofit`](https://github.com/polysites/polysite-themes/tree/main/polysite-theme-nonprofit).

## Fernleaf

![Fernleaf: the home page of the helpdesk example theme](/examples/helpdesk.png)

A knowledge base: a banner with the site search, topic cards, a sidebar of topics on article pages, FAQs and a contact form. Theme: [`polysite-theme-helpdesk`](https://github.com/polysites/polysite-themes/tree/main/polysite-theme-helpdesk).

## Use one, or make your own

A site picks its theme in the admin (**Sites**, then **Theme**), so two sites on one installation can look completely different. Start from the
starter theme with `scripts/new-theme.sh` and follow the [theme developer guide](https://github.com/polysites/polysite-themes/tree/main/docs).
See [Themes](/guide/themes) for how themes work. A few blocks in the examples need the `blocks-pack` plugin (see [Plugins](/guide/plugins)).
